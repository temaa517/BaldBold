## Brand & Style

This design system crafts an unapologetic, sharp, and sophisticated digital storefront for confident head-shavers. Rejecting clinical insecurity and self-effacing humor, the aesthetic pairs raw masculine refinement with high-fashion self-assurance and witty candor. The target demographic values grooming as deliberate style rather than quiet compromise.

The design movement blends **Warm Minimalism** with **High-Contrast Modernism**:
- Deep graphite anchors the visual weight, avoiding sterile pitch-black in favor of structured richness.
- Warm sand and golden amber inject warmth, tactile craft, and a sunlit glow.
- Tactile surfaces rely on expansive negative space, hyper-legible geometric display typography, and smooth, refined corners that echo polished, sculpted silhouettes.

## Colors

The palette leverages high contrast to balance precision with warmth:

- **Primary (`#1E1E24`)**: Dark Graphite. The foundational base for primary typography, impactful CTA surfaces, and authoritative high-contrast elements.
- **Secondary (`#E8D5B7`)**: Warm Sand. A refined, tactile neutral used for premium highlights, promotional badges, subtle banner backdrops, and packaging callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#F2A93B`)**: Accent Amber. The core energetic signal representing shine, vitality, and action. Reserved for interactive focal points, review stars, and alert states.
- **Accent Hover (`#D88F23`)**: Deepened Amber. Provides grounded state feedback on interactive highlights.
- **Surfaces**:
  - Pure White (`#FFFFFF`): Primary card surfaces and clean interactive input containers.
  - Soft Gray (`#F4F4F6`): Canvas wash to make pure white modules and cards lift smoothly.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E2E2E6`)**: Subtle Slate. Creates gentle boundary containment without breaking page rhythm.
- **Muted Text (`#6B7280`)**: Secondary metadata, SKU details, breadcrumbs, and helper text.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances punchy bravado with utilitarian clarity:

- **Headlines (Montserrat)**: Bold geometric proportions project swagger and clean architectural strength. Headlines are tight, structured, and set with slightly negative letter tracking at scale to create immediate impact in hero sections and witty editorial taglines.
- **Body & Controls (Inter)**: High legibility, neutral grotesk architecture, and disciplined vertical metrics ensure frictionless e-commerce browsing, ingredient readability, and clear transactional paths.
- **Editorial Voice**: Combine large-scale Montserrat display moments with understated Inter body narratives to allow punchy one-liners to lead the visual flow.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout adheres to a structured 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to elevate merchandise and lifestyle portraiture:

- **Desktop (12 Columns)**: 1200px to 1440px max-width container, 48px canvas margins, and 24px gutters. Product listings resolve across 3 or 4 columns; content stories collapse into 6-column alternating splits.
- **Tablet (8 Columns)**: 32px canvas margins, 20px gutters. Grids adapt to 2 columns for products and dual columns for comparison matrices.
- **Mobile (4 Columns)**: 20px canvas margins, 16px gutters. Horizontal card carousels overflow smoothly off-canvas to encourage gestural browsing.
- **Spacing Scale**: Strict multiples of 4px and 8px govern internal padding, keeping vertical rhythm locked across editorial hero banners, product configurations, and checkout flows.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with airy restraint to keep the catalog crisp and grounded:

- **Ambient Elevation**: Depth is primarily communicated through a single, diffuse shadow formula: `0 4px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`. This creates floating cards without dramatic, dirty edges.
- **Tonal Layering**: Elevated layers sit on `#FFFFFF` surfaces against the `#F4F4F6` background. Borders remain hairline-subtle (`1px solid #E2E2E6`) to hold visual boundaries when shadows are turned off in dense data layouts (e.g., checkout order summaries).
- **Hover Micro-Lifts**: Interactive cards translate `-2px` on the Y-axis on desktop hover while shadow diffusion expands to `0 8px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`, creating a tactile response.

## Shapes

The shape system relies on deliberate curvature that mirrors smooth, sculpted grooming tools:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Bound to `16px` radius (`rounded-lg`), delivering a cushioned, ergonomic silhouette that softens high-contrast typography.
- **Buttons & Interactive Inputs**: Bound to `12px` radius for standard actions, balancing precision touch targets with soft edges.
- **Badges & Pills**: Full circular pill geometry (`9999px`) reserved strictly for status chips, product tags ("Matte Finish", "SPF 50"), and review count pills.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#1E1E24`, text `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `12px`, padding `14px 28px`. Hover shifts to `#2E2E36`. Active state scales to `0.98`.
- **Accent (Checkout / Hero CTA)**: Background `#F2A93B`, text `#1E1E24` (SemiBold), border-radius `12px`. Hover shifts to `#D88F23`.
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1.5px border `#E2E2E6`, text `#1E1E24`, background transparent. Hover sets background to `#FFFFFF` with border `#1E1E24`.

### Cards (Product & Editorial)
- Base background `#FFFFFF`, border-radius `16px`, border `1px solid #E2E2E6`, shadow `0 4px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- Image frames inside cards feature an inner subtle `#F4F4F6` backing with `12px` nested radius.
- Padding inside cards defaults to `space-lg` (24px).

### Chips & Badges
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `6px 12px`.
- Feature tags: Background `#E8D5B7`, text `#1E1E24`, font weight 600, typography `label-sm`.
- Accent tags ("Best for Shine", "Anti-Glare"): Background `#F2A93B` at 15% opacity with solid `#1E1E24` text.

### Form Inputs & Search
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E2E6`, border-radius `12px`, height `48px`, padding `0 16px`.
- Focus state: Border color transitions to `#1E1E24` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 30, 36, 0.1)`.
- Placeholder text in `#6B7280`.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: 20x20px with `6px` radius. Active fill `#1E1E24` with `#FFFFFF` checkmark icon.
- Radios: Circular 20x20px. Active state displays an inner solid dot of `#1E1E24` with a 4px inset white ring.

### Iconography
- Line-art style with a uniform `1.75px` stroke, rounded caps, and rounded joins.
- Sized on 20px and 24px bounding boxes to preserve geometric harmony alongside typography.

### Specialized E-Commerce Modules
- **Scalp Shimmer/Matte Meter**: Dual-spectrum selector using a segmented slider with an amber pill thumb indicator.
- **Subscription Cadence Selector**: Bordered multi-option cards showing refill frequencies, flagged by `#E8D5B7` "Save 15%" badges.
